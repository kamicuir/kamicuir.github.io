Recommendation

Order a sample from D—— and A—— before committing. Both are real factories, both are inside budget at 1,000 pcs, and D—— gives a free sample so the only cost is shipping.

The two trading companies are not automatically worse — C—— is the right choice if the buyer needs 100 pcs to test a market. But for a 1,000 pc order with custom logo, going direct to the factory removes roughly ¥0.9–1.5 per unit.

How the factory check was done

  • Business licence cross-checked against the company name on the 1688 profile
  • Profile inspected for production floor photos, equipment lists and years active — trading companies usually show a warehouse instead
  • Product range checked: a real factory sells a narrow range. A company listing 40 unrelated categories is reselling
  • Asked directly for a short video of the production line — factories send one, traders change the subject
  • Compared the quote against the category average on 1688 to catch padded pricing
