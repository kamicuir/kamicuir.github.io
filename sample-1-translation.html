Sample 1 — Chinese → English Product Copy

Three real listings rewritten for an English-speaking buyer. Source copy is from mainland e-commerce listings; the English is localized, not translated word-for-word.

Project A — Vacuum Insulated Bottle

Original (Chinese)

304不锈钢内胆，24小时保温保冷，一键弹盖设计，单手可开，容量500ml，重量仅280g，杯身磨砂防滑，适合办公、户外、车载使用。

Delivered (English)

500ml Vacuum Insulated Bottle

  • 304 food-grade stainless steel liner — hot for 24 hours, cold for 24 hours
  • One-touch flip lid, opens with one hand
  • 500ml capacity, weighs just 280g
  • Matte non-slip body
  • Built for the office, the trail and the car cup holder
Translator notes
保温保冷Split into "hot for 24 hours, cold for 24 hours" — a single English phrase would lose the duration promise.
一键弹盖"One-touch flip lid" instead of "one-key bounce cover". The literal version reads like a machine part.
适合办公、户外、车载使用"Built for the office, the trail and the car cup holder" — the preposition "built for" carries the benefit, not just the compatibility.

Project B — Wireless Earbuds

Original (Chinese)

采用蓝牙5.3芯片，连接更稳定，传输距离达10米；双麦克风降噪通话，地铁上也能听清；充电仓总续航32小时，单次听歌6小时；支持触控操作，IPX5级防水，运动出汗不担心。

Delivered (English)

Wireless Earbuds with Bluetooth 5.3

  • Bluetooth 5.3 chipset — steadier connection, 10m range
  • Dual-mic noise cancellation: clear calls even on a subway
  • 32 hours total with the charging case, 6 hours per charge
  • Touch controls
  • IPX5 water resistance — sweat is not a problem
Translator notes
信号更稳定Chinese listings promise "更稳定". English buyers read "steadier" as a comparison, so it needs a baseline — "steadier connection" is enough without an invented number.
地铁上也能听清Kept the subway detail. It is the single most persuasive line in the source copy.
IPX5级防水Grade kept as-is; the sentence was shifted to the outcome ("sweat is not a problem") because the grade alone means nothing to a shopper.

Project C — Yoga Mat

Original (Chinese)

TPE环保材质，无异味，加厚8mm，双面防滑纹理，保护膝盖和脊椎；附带背带，方便携带；适合瑜伽、普拉提、健身垫多种场景。

Delivered (English)

8mm TPE Yoga Mat

  • TPE foam, no rubber smell
  • 8mm thick — cushions your knees and spine
  • Double-sided non-slip texture
  • Carry strap included
  • Works as a yoga, Pilates or general fitness mat
Translator notes
环保材质"Eco-friendly material" is near-meaningless in English listings. Replaced with the concrete, checkable promise: "no rubber smell".
保护膝盖和脊椎"Cushions" instead of "protects" — English buyers read "protects" as a medical claim and distrust it.
多种场景Named the scenes instead of the abstract word "scenarios".
